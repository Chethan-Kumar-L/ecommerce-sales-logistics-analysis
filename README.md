# 📦 E-Commerce Sales & Logistics Analysis (Excel)

## 🎯 Objective
Evaluated regional sales performance, delivery lead times, and fulfillment efficiency to identify operational bottlenecks impacting profitability and customer satisfaction.

## 🛠️ Tools & Techniques
* **Spreadsheet Software:** Microsoft Excel
* **Functions & Features:** `XLOOKUP`, Dynamic Pivot Tables, Conditional Formatting
* **Data Visualization:** Regional Bar Charts, Delivery Status Donut Charts, Trend Lines

## 📊 Key Findings & Business Insights
* **Revenue Concentration:** Generated strong sales in the North ($10,491), West ($8,359), and South ($7,317) regions.
* **Cancellation Impact:** Discovered a concerning **30% overall cancellation rate**, peaking at 40% in the North region, resulting in a 27% loss of potential revenue.
* **Fulfillment Bottlenecks:** Found that only **11% of orders** were delivered within the 2-day SLA, with an average delivery time of 3.76 days.

## 💡 Strategic Recommendations
1. **Optimize Logistics:** Introduce stricter delivery SLAs and track fulfillment partner performance to reduce lead times.
2. **Investigate Cancellations:** Conduct root-cause analysis on high cancellation zones (especially the North region) and verify inventory before order dispatch.
3. **Product Focus:** Expand inventory and marketing for high-performing categories like *Home* and *Electronics*.
